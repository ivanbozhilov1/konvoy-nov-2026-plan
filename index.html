Words we use in this plan

EV4 / EV5: EV4 is this event, 26 Nov. EV5 is the next one, announced in the cage.

Pilot: our first 16 shorts (5–12 Nov): 4 finishes, 4 face-offs, 4 callouts, 4 emotional. It tests what works.

COUNTDOWN: a daily series of finish clips counting down to fight night. #1, our best finish, goes out on 25 Nov.

Cap: the most videos one account may post per day. It’s our own limit to avoid looking like spam.

Package: the exact video file, caption, thumbnail, audio and target account, approved as one unit.

Approved to Publish / Needs Review: statuses in our Content Calendar. Any change sends a package back to Needs Review.

Trial Reel / Collab: Trial = an Instagram Reel shown to non-followers first. Collab = co-posted with a fighter or gym.

Priority 1–2: the top-ranked contacts in our 1,678-record invite and partner directory.

Checked / partial: checked = identity and contact confirmed from a dated source. Partial = name matched but not confirmed yet.

Wave 1 / Wave 2: outreach batches. Wave 1 = 265 checked contacts. Wave 2 = 210 partials, once confirmed.

Declaration / CEO’s Eye: CEO series: “something big is coming” posts, then 60–90 s fighter breakdowns.

[Owner], [X], [TBD]: placeholders. We fill them in on Tuesday.

Why caps: posting too much, too similar, gets accounts throttled. We raise a cap only when 2 days of approved clips are waiting and the account check is clean. Plus 1 photo or poster a day.

Every morning: check each account’s status page. If flagged: lower caps for 7 days, pause outreach for 48 h, never re-upload the flagged clip. DMs: ≤10 new a day, no link in the first message. Fighters, gyms and press go by email or phone.
